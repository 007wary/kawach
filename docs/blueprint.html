Product blueprint · v1 · 30 Oct 2026 · Assam, India

Kawach & Surokkha

Two safety apps. Kawach stops scams aimed at elders by bringing a trusted family member into the loop within seconds. Surokkha makes every ride in a shared auto, e-rickshaw or city bus traceable for women in small cities. Each section below has screen mockups, features, architecture, data model, a 12-month roadmap, costs, metrics and risks.

Phase 1 costs nothing: the existing Play Console account, free tiers only, Vercel Hobby with a .vercel.app address, and your own phone for testing. App 1 · Kawach

A scam shield with a family guardian mode

Scams work through urgency and isolation. Kawach breaks the isolation: the elder taps one button, a family member sees the message within seconds, and the app explains the scam in plain words in the elder's own language.

Who it's for

Users and the job each one needs done

  • Elder (60+): "Tell me quickly whether this is real, in my language, without making me feel foolish."
  • Guardian (25–45, often in another city): "Let me know before Maa pays, not after."
  • Who pays: the guardian, through a family plan. Banks and cooperatives later pay per protected customer.
  • Launch market: Guwahati, plus families from Assam working in Bengaluru, Delhi and Pune.
How the check works

Message to verdict in under a second

  • Layer 1 · rules (MVP): runs on the phone, works offline, costs nothing. About 60 keyword and number patterns, such as "digital arrest", "KYC expire", "collect request", fake short links and +92/+855 country codes. Each scam type has a short written explanation in Assamese, Hindi and English.
  • Layer 2 · people (MVP): the guardian always sees the message. Their "safe" or "scam" reply is saved and used to improve the patterns each week.
  • Layer 3 · AI check (Phase 3): an LLM handles messages the rules can't decide. Phone and account numbers are removed before sending.
  • Voice explanations (Phase 3): added once the text version is proven.

App 2 · Surokkha

A trip shield for informal transport

In a shared auto or e-rickshaw there's no ride app, no driver record and no GPS trail. Surokkha logs the vehicle in about 2 seconds, shares the trip live with trusted contacts, and builds a safety map made by women commuters.

Who it's for

Users and the job each one needs done

  • Rider (16–45): students, nurses, retail and office staff travelling after dark. "If something happens, someone must know exactly which vehicle I was in."
  • Trusted contact: parent, friend or partner. Watches through a link, no app needed.
  • Other riders: see how many times a vehicle has been logged and whether it has reports, from plate number alone.
  • Who pays: colleges, employers with night shifts, city police and CSR. Free for riders.
Design rules

Built for the moment of fear

  • Start a trip in 2 seconds: camera opens straight to the plate scanner.
  • Works on bad networks: SMS fallback for SOS; GPS points are stored on the phone and sent once the network returns.
  • Discreet: the SOS can run silently. "Calculator" disguise mode in phase 3.
  • Privacy: trip GPS is deleted after 7 days unless an SOS happened. Map reports never show who sent them.

Tech stack

Same base as Kawach, plus maps

  • App: Flutter, background location service (Android foreground service).
  • Backend: Supabase free tier: Postgres + PostGIS for geo queries, Realtime for live trips, Google sign-in.
  • Maps: MapLibre with a free tile plan (MapTiler or Stadia free tier). The public OpenStreetMap tile server doesn't allow app traffic.
  • SMS: sent from the rider's own SIM, so no gateway bill. Automatic sending needs Google Play's SMS permission approval; until then the app opens a pre-filled SMS and she taps Send.
  • OCR: Google ML Kit (on device, free).
  • Watchdog: Supabase scheduled job (pg_cron, free) every minute checks open trips.
  • Contact link + dashboards: Next.js on Vercel Hobby.

From one campus to the Northeast

  • Survey 200 women commuters (Cotton University, Gauhati University, Dispur offices, hospital nursing staff).

Risks

What could go wrong, and the plan for it

RiskAppMitigation
Android blocks SMS/call-log accessKawachMVP uses the share menu and paste only; call screening uses the official CallScreeningService API.
Wrong "safe" verdict on a real scamKawachNever say "100% safe"; say "no known scam pattern, check with family". The guardian is always told.
Elders find it confusingKawachOne button, large text, the guardian sets it up; test with 5 elders before every release. Voice comes in Phase 3 for elders who don't read easily.
Rules miss new scam wordingKawachThe guardian always sees the message, so a human is the backup; new patterns added weekly from the admin panel without an app update.
Supabase free project pausesBothFree projects pause after 7 days without activity. Real users keep it awake; during quiet testing, open the app or run a small daily ping.
Play blocks automatic SOS SMSSurokkhaPush alerts are the main channel. SMS opens pre-filled for her to send; apply for the SMS permission exception later.
Battery drain from GPSSurokkhaGPS runs only during a trip; 15-second interval; foreground service.
Fake or abusive map reportsSurokkhaModeration queue, rate limits, a report needs 3+ independent reports before showing.
False sense of securitySurokkhaClear copy: "Surokkha alerts your contacts; it is not the police. Call 112 in danger."
Personal data law (DPDP Act 2023)BothExplicit consent screens, data minimisation, auto-delete, privacy policy, servers in India (Supabase Mumbai region).
Recommendation

Build Kawach first (weeks 1–8), entirely on free tiers, then reuse its login, push, Supabase and Flutter base for Surokkha, which cuts about 3 weeks from its build. Use AssamJobDesk to recruit beta families and the 12 closed-test testers. Start Surokkha's field validation (surveys and police meetings) while Kawach is in beta. Spend money only when revenue or a grant arrives, in this order: AI check, Vercel Pro + domain, voice.
