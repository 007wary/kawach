Kawach

Check a message before you pay.

Kawach is an Android app that spots common scams in SMS and WhatsApp messages — digital arrest, fake KYC, UPI collect requests, lottery and job scams — and lets an elder ask family with one tap.

Works in English, हिन्दी and অসমীয়া. Checks run on the phone, offline and free.

Already paid a scammer? Call the cyber fraud helpline 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in.
