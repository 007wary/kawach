Delete your account and data

Kawach, by Mwnswrang Wary. Last updated: 31 October 2026

Message checks run on your phone and are not stored by us. We only store data if you use family alerts. This page explains how to delete it.

If you are the family member (signed in with Google)

  1. Email 007mwnswrangwary@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with. Use the subject line "Delete my Kawach account".
  2. We delete your account and data within 30 days, and reply to confirm.

If you are the protected person (joined with a code)

  1. In Kawach, open Family alerts and tap Remove next to each family member. This deletes the links straight away.
  2. Uninstall Kawach.
  3. Optional, to delete everything sooner: email 007mwnswrangwary@gmail.com with the name you used in Kawach and your family member's email address. We delete your data within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: the Google name and email for family members, or the app ID for protected people.
  • Your family links, and the names shown to your family.
  • Messages sent to family for checking, and the "safe" or "scam" replies.
  • Your language setting and the device token used for alerts.

What is kept, and for how long

Nothing is kept after deletion. Even without a deletion request, messages sent to family are deleted automatically after 30 days. Server logs at our hosting providers (Google Firebase, Vercel) expire on their own schedule, usually within 30 days.
