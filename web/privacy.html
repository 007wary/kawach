Kawach

Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

Kawach is an Android app that helps people spot scam messages and ask their family for help. It is developed by Mwnswrang Wary in Assam, India. Contact: 007mwnswrangwary@gmail.com.

Messages you check

When you paste or share a message into Kawach, the check runs on your phone. The message is not sent to us. If you link family members, the message (with phone and account numbers masked) and the result are sent to the family members you chose, so they can help you. Stored copies are deleted automatically after 30 days.

If you check a screenshot or photo, Kawach reads the words in it on your phone, using Google ML Kit through Google Play services. The picture itself is never uploaded, not even to your family. Only the words read from it are treated like a pasted message, as described above. Google Play services may send Google basic diagnostic data about how the text reader performs; it does not send the picture or its words.

What we collect for family alerts

  • Family members (guardians): name and email from Google sign-in.
  • People who are protected: the name you type when linking (for example "Maa"), shown only to your linked family.
  • All users: an app ID, your chosen language, which accounts are linked as family, and a device token used only to deliver alerts.

Kawach does not read your SMS, contacts, call log or other apps on its own. It only sees what you choose to share with it, including a single picture you pick.

How data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase in Mumbai, India (asia-south1) and sent over encrypted connections. Push alerts are delivered by Firebase Cloud Messaging.

What we never do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with anyone except the family members you link, and service providers needed to run the app (Google Firebase, Vercel).

Your choices

You can unlink family members in the app at any time. To delete your account and all data, email 007mwnswrangwary@gmail.com from your registered email, and we will delete it within 30 days. Full steps are on the delete your account page. Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your phone.

Children

Kawach is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and show a notice in the app.
